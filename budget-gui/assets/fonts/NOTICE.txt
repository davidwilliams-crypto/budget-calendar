Geist Tabular

These fonts are a Modified Version of Geist 1.800 (Regular, Medium, SemiBold) by The Geist Project
Authors, https://github.com/vercel/geist-font, commit ef2597cfc42f4797676df105517ccc0a1cfa6569.
They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt in this folder).

The only change: the digits 0-9 map straight to Geist's tabular figures (its "tnum" glyphs), because
the app's text engine cannot switch OpenType features on, and money columns need figures of equal
width. The family is renamed "Geist Tabular" so it is not mistaken for the original.

Budget Calendar embeds them in its executable. The Meridian design system sets all text in Geist.

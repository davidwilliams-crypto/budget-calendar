# Budget Calendar UI

## Visual system

The desktop interface uses Segoe UI, a restrained blue/slate palette, 14 px body and button text, 12 px captions, clear headings, consistent 34–36 px controls, and 10 px card corners. User-customized budget colors remain supported. Income and expense colors emphasize amounts; current-day and selection states use blue.

The application has a calendar brand mark and matching SVG, PNG, and Windows ICO assets in `budget-gui/assets`. The native window uses the PNG; the desktop shortcut uses the ICO.

## Navigation and layout

- The header identifies the app, current version, file and saved state. Click the version to open build details and dated change history; View also contains About and version history. New payment is the primary action. Save changes is disabled while everything is saved and is raised to the accent (accent outline and text) while there are unsaved changes. With no budget open, neither New payment nor Save changes is shown; the empty state offers Open… instead. File contains Open, Save as, recent budgets and Close. View contains appearance, calendar cents, breakdown visibility and shortcut hints. Reports lists the printable and spreadsheet reports (see Reports below).
- Calendar and Payments are persistent workspaces in the same window. Switching pages retains the document and search/selection state.
- Calendar navigation offers previous/next month, a month/year chooser, and Today on wider windows. Search transactions filters immediately; advanced expressions such as `cat:Auto` and `<-100` remain optional. Invalid filters receive a readable explanation.
- A compact financial strip shows opening balance, money in, money out and closing balance with cents. Hover for the exact value, net change and minimum, average and maximum balances. Oversized values use an explicit ellipsis and remain available in tooltips and accessibility information.
- The month grid fits all four, five or six weeks in the available window without vertical scrolling. Each payment is an 18px single-line row, with a readable regular 14px name on the left and a semibold 13px amount aligned on the right, so the amounts carry the weight when scanning a busy week. Separate columns prevent overlap; long names truncate and remain available on hover. Week heights respond to the busiest day so quiet weeks give room to busy ones. The ordinary five-week budget's 4–5 payments per busy date fit at 1200×800. Smaller windows show several rows where space permits; +N in the day header opens every transaction if overflow remains. Current day is blue; minimum/maximum/low balances use small markers. By default, the highest balance uses a green strip and the lowest uses a blue one, and the low-balance warning stays amber. Custom budget colours still override these.
- The optional monthly breakdown appears at widths of at least 1480 px. Its share column shows unsigned percentages with a unit (`29%`), so a share never reads like a second amount; only a negative disposable income keeps its minus sign. The bars carry the income/expense colour. The caption names the base: "% of income", or "% of expenses" for a month without income. The header and financial summary use compact spacing to prioritize the calendar. The app initially maximizes to the available work area; explicit CLI sizes remain available for captures.
- The footer spans the full window width, under the monthly breakdown as well as the calendar. It shows the document count, the search result count when relevant, the save state and keyboard hints.
- A budget that cannot be opened shows a banner titled "Couldn't open <file>". Under the title is one readable cause, such as "Line 23: Invalid data for 'frequency'", followed by an Open another… action. The full loader log sits under a collapsed Details section.
- Like the original, the app checks the local date every 20 seconds, also while idle. After midnight the new day becomes today without asking: the today marker, tentative payments and date-relative searches move to it. If the current month was on screen the calendar follows into a new month (31 October → 1 November); a month you are browsing stays on screen. `--today` keeps the date fixed.

## Version history

The native title and header show the running app version. The version-history dialog shows a deterministic build identifier, dated release notes from `CHANGELOG.md`, and Copy version info. The dialog scrolls within compact windows, and closing it preserves any unfinished budget form.

Version 0.3.0 is the first tracked local release. Every delivered application update receives a new version and changelog entry. `scripts/archive-release.ps1` preserves the executable, icon, notes, verified matching source ZIP, build-input manifest and SHA256 records under `local-releases/vVERSION`; it refuses to replace an existing version with different build contents. `AGENTS.md` records the release workflow for subsequent work.

## Payments workspace

Payments is a full page with live description/category search, All/Enabled/Disabled filters, numeric and date/name sorting, and aligned virtualized rows. All records, including historical ones, are accessible by default. Enabled means not disabled; it does not hide ended or future records.

Rows show description/category, amount, a readable recurrence and start date (`Since 2 Jan 2026` for past starts, `Starts 5 Dec 2027` for future ones), and an overflow menu with confirmed deletion. The status column marks only the exception: a muted **Disabled** badge; enabled rows leave it empty. The **Edit** button appears only on the hovered or selected row (its space is reserved, so the ⋯ menu never moves); double-click edits any row. The ⋯ menu uses plain menu items, with **Delete payment…** in the negative colour, as in the calendar context menus. Selection stays tied to the record when sorting/filtering. Empty and no-results states offer useful recovery actions. Ctrl+P opens Payments and focuses search.

## Editing

New payment / Ctrl+N opens grouped payment and schedule fields. Expense and Income are explicit choices; enter the amount as a positive number. Existing signed amounts and hidden payment metadata are preserved. Start/end dates support both ISO typing and a calendar picker. Choosing a recurring schedule from Once defaults to continuing indefinitely; subsequent end-condition choices are retained. As in the original dialog, a payment that ends "When loan is paid" cannot be Tentative, and a Tentative payment cannot end when a loan is paid or move weekend payments to a business day (those controls are disabled, with a short explanation). The category cannot be the same as the payment's description.

Editing an existing payment (Edit whole payment, or from the Payments page) changes every occurrence. When the amount or schedule of a payment that started before today changes, the first Apply shows a notice naming the first affected date and pointing to the transaction editor's **This and all future occurrences** scope; Apply to all occurrences then confirms. Renames and other changes that leave the amount and schedule alone apply at once.

Click a transaction to edit that date's amount, note and status. Right-click for series editing/deletion and filtering. Click +N more for a day dialog containing every transaction. While a search is active, +N counts only the hidden matching transactions; the day dialog then lists the matching transactions first ("2 of 5 transactions match the search") and the rest under **Other transactions on this day**. To add a payment on a date, hover the day and click the **+** that appears in its header (tooltip "Add payment on <date>"), double-click an empty part of the day, or right-click the day. Editing an entry from the day dialog returns to the dialog with refreshed amounts, as Delete does. Loan changes recalculate payoff/future installments, and marking a tentative occurrence Paid pins it to that date (it no longer slides to the current day) and updates the period's unpaid estimates; forms explain these native behaviors.

A day with a deleted or moved-away transaction shows a restore icon; its tooltip names those payments. Right-click the day (or open its day dialog) and choose **Restore '<name>'** to bring the occurrence back, as in the original. Restoring is disabled while a search filter is active. The original's leading `-` "deleted transactions" filter is not supported; typing it shows a hint pointing to Restore instead of silently ignoring the `-`.

Clicking a transaction opens its editor on the first click; if that click was the start of a double-click, the second click is ignored by the editor (it cannot press Delete or change the status) and the amount stays selected for typing.

The amount scope offers **Only this occurrence** (default) or **This and all future occurrences**. Future scope applies the amount from the selected date onward, including future amount exceptions, while keeping earlier amounts unchanged. Notes and status remain specific to the selected date. Deleted occurrences stay deleted and moved dates remain in place. Delete transaction still affects one occurrence.

Drag a payment row onto a date to move that occurrence, including adjacent-month dates. Hold over a month arrow (or press PgUp/PgDn) to navigate while dragging; shortcuts that open dialogs or switch views wait until the drag ends. Drops on the same date or outside the calendar do nothing; a destination that already contains the same payment is rejected. Right-click a day balance or the opening/closing summary balance and choose Edit balance to set an absolute closing balance for the displayed date. Later balances recalculate up to the next checkpoint; an existing adjustment can be removed.

Forms have grouped fields, visible scrolling, pinned validation, and one footer layout that fits compact windows: an optional destructive ghost action on the left (the transaction editor's **Delete occurrence…**, with a trash icon in the negative colour), then Cancel beside the primary action on the right. Each caption sits 4px above its control and fields are 12px apart (16px before a section), so captions read as belonging to their field. Expense/Income is a two-segment control styled like the Calendar/Payments tabs; the selected segment and the 34px amount text take the negative (Expense) or positive (Income) colour. Date fields pair the ISO input with a 36px calendar icon button (tooltip "Pick date") so they line up with full-width fields. The date picker uses real, bounded calendar dates and starts the week on the same day as the calendar grid (`firstDayOfWeekIsSunday`). Validation never commits invalid drafts.

## Saving and protection

Apply changes in memory, then Save changes / Ctrl+S to write the budget. A clean document is not rewritten. The title, badge and footer indicate applied unsaved changes.

Opening a different budget or closing prompts to save, discard or cancel; an unapplied draft must first be finished or explicitly discarded. Failed/cancelled Save as keeps the current path and unsaved state. Disk changes made by another app are detected, also when Save as targets the open file under a different spelling; Save as can keep edits separately. If the open budget was deleted or moved away, Save writes it again at its original location. Budgets are remembered in `.budgetrc` by absolute path, and View preferences are written when the app closes; `--screenshot` runs leave `.budgetrc` untouched. A rejected password clears the field and keeps keyboard focus there. Typing a year in the month chooser navigates once the year is committed (Enter or leaving the field).

Writes stage the full file and retain the previous version as `.bak`, then `.bak.1`, `.bak.2`, etc. The newest 10 backups are kept; older ones are removed after a successful save, and numbering continues upward so higher numbers are always newer. Existing per-date exceptions, notes, encryption and balance adjustments remain supported.

## Reports

The header's **Reports** menu (beside View and File) is disabled until a budget is open. **Print this month** opens the printed calendar of the month on screen at once. The other items keep the original's names in two captioned groups: *Income vs. Expenses* (Print…, Excel Report…, Comparative report to…, Month-by-Month report…) and *Transactions* (Print Monthly Calendar…, Monthly Detailed Report…, Monthly Excel Report…, Unpaid & paid Report…). Each opens a compact range dialog in the style of the other forms: a badge naming the group and where the report opens, From and To month/year choosers (months outside the budget's scheduled period are disabled), **This month** and **Last 12 months** shortcuts, a line stating exactly which months or dates the report covers, and the shared Cancel / Create report footer. The fields scroll in compact windows while a validation message and the footer stay pinned. Enter creates the report; Escape cancels.

As in the original Stats window the range starts at the month on screen; Month-by-Month starts with the last 12 months because it needs at least two. The Comparative report adds **Compare with**: a start month whose range has the same length as the base range (its To month is shown read-only), defaulting to the previous period, with **Previous period** and **A year earlier** shortcuts. Range rules follow the original: the Income vs. Expenses tables and the printed calendar include both From and To; Unpaid & paid runs from the 1st of From to the last day of To; Monthly Detailed and Monthly Excel also include the To month (the original left it out). From after To, a one-month Month-by-Month report, a comparison that overlaps the base range (the original never offered one) and months outside the budget are refused with a message naming the months. Reports ignore the calendar search.

Each report is written to a new `bgt-report-*.html` in the system temporary folder, never beside the budget, with its graph bars (in the `.budgetrc` graph colours) and day-note icon embedded so the file stands alone; payment pictures are left blank because the original's `images/` folder is not part of the port. HTML reports open in the default browser. As in the original, the two Excel reports are HTML too and open with the program registered for `.xls` (or `.xlsx`); without one those items are disabled. The file is handed to Windows directly, so no command line is built and paths need no quoting. A report that cannot be created, saved or opened is explained in the banner (naming the saved file when only opening failed); success shows a short confirmation. The temporary files are left for Windows to clean up.

## Verification and captures

CLI: `budget-gui budget.bgt --screenshot out.png --size 1200x800 [--dark]`.

The redesign was checked with a copied budget in standard, compact, wide, and dark layouts, including validation, repeat controls, date selection, live search, and transaction/day dialogs. The original budget is not test data. Historical capture references in earlier design notes predate this redesign.

## Dialog keys

In the payment, transaction and balance dialogs Enter in a single-line field applies the form, and Escape cancels (discarding the draft) unless a dropdown or date picker is open, which closes first. Clicking the backdrop keeps the dialog open. The read-only dialogs (day transactions, calendar legend, keyboard shortcuts, version history) close with Escape, their Done/Close button or a click on the dimmed backdrop. With a day dialog's More menu open, Escape or a click outside the menu closes only the menu; the second click of the double-click that opened the dialog does not close it. Amount fields accept thousands commas, a leading `$`, and accounting parentheses, up to 1,000,000,000,000. They also accept calculator formulas (`+ - * / ( )` and `k`/`m`/`b`/`t` multipliers, e.g. `1200-350` or `2.5k`); `%n` income percentages are refused because these fields have no income context, and more than 64 nested parentheses are refused as "Expression too deeply nested". Existing amounts are shown with two decimals (`1,100.00`); leaving the box unchanged keeps the exact stored amount, even a legacy one above the maximum.

## Theme and shell refinements

- Calendar cells have three distinct tiers: past days, weekends and leading/trailing days of adjacent months differ visibly in both themes. Day numbers recede to muted in light mode so payment names dominate.
- All text roles (text, muted, faint, positive, negative, disabled, balance strips) meet WCAG AA 4.5:1 on the surface; a unit test enforces this. Buttons show a 2px accent focus ring, accent hover borders and a pointing-hand cursor.
- Income rows carry a 3px left accent bar in the income colour, so paydays stand out; expense rows have no bar and show the U+2212 minus, so polarity does not depend on colour alone. Names are regular and every amount is semibold.
- An adjusted (reconciled) day balance shows `′` after the number, followed by the adjustment in parentheses when it is at least 1, e.g. `1,250′(250)` (the parenthesis is dropped in cells too narrow for it); its tooltip lists the balance before adjustment and the adjustment amount.
- A transaction with a note shows a small pencil before its name. A disabled payment keeps its highlight colour at a lighter tint, with muted text.
- Ordinary balance strips show only the right-aligned number on a faint tint. A balance that repeats the previous day's is faint; a day whose balance moved shows it in full-ink semibold, so changes stand out (the faint role also meets 4.5:1 on the strip tint). Lowest, highest and below-minimum days get a labelled, fully tinted strip (Low shows a warning glyph).
- The Closing balance tile is the hero (accent border and tint, larger value, arrow with the net change since opening). Money in/out tiles have a coloured left edge.
- Header: brand, then a file icon and name, then a status pill (green dot Saved, amber Unsaved changes). The version chip is quiet text.
- Calendar/Payments segmented tabs have icons and an accent underline. Today is an accent button only when another month is shown.
- Empty budgets show an "Add your first payment" card over the grid. With no file open, a dashed drop zone and recent-file cards (with last-modified age) are shown. Each recent file is checked once, in the background: its card reads "Checking…" until the answer arrives, so a budget on an unreachable network share never freezes the window. At startup the last budget opens as soon as its check finds it, unless you open something else first.
- Dropping a budget opens it (after the usual unsaved-changes prompt). While a dialog is open the "Drop to open" target is not shown, and a budget dropped anyway leaves the dialog alone and shows a brief "Close the open dialog first" notice.
- Summary panel: percentages are shares of total income, or of total expenses when the (filtered) month has no income, and `?` when it has neither (original rule); the 5% fold into Other… uses the same base. Total income shows its difference from plan in the "vs plan" column when it is at least 5. Share bars are 4px full-width tracks, only filterable rows highlight on hover, shares are unsigned with a % unit (only a negative disposable income shows the U+2212 minus), and the disposable-income rule is coloured by sign.
- Keyboard: F1 or ? opens the shortcuts sheet (also View > Keyboard shortcuts). New: Alt+Left/Right change month (also while the search box has focus) and T jumps to the current month. Footer hints use keycap chips.
- Tab order on the Calendar page: header actions and Calendar/Payments, then the search box, month navigation, the four summary tiles (each a focusable control with a 2px accent ring) and the calendar's payment rows and +N buttons, day by day; Shift+Tab goes back, and Tab wraps from the last row to the header. At widths with the monthly breakdown, its rows come before the summary tiles. A focused payment row shows a 2px accent ring inside its unchanged row; Enter or Space opens the same editor as a click, and closing a dialog returns focus to the control that opened it. Day cells and balance strips stay mouse targets (double-click and right-click menus) and are not Tab stops.
- Outer gaps (header, summary strip, grid, footer) are all 8px.
- Today's cell has a stronger state: 7% (light) / 12% (dark) accent tint, a 1px accent inset border, a 2px accent bar along the top edge, and a bold day number in a 17px accent pill that fits inside the day header (it widens for two-digit dates). Row geometry is unchanged.
- Row markers are 6px coloured dots with a 1px halo (visible on dark): status (Paid green, Important orange) at the left of the name, and after the amount "adjusted" (accent: the amount or date differs from the regular schedule, i.e. a changed amount, a moved transaction or a weekend shift) or "last payment" (muted). The apostrophe/asterisk text suffix is gone from the amount. View > Calendar legend explains each marker.
- The row hover tooltip is a compact card: bold name, category and status pills (Paid, Important, Disabled, Adjusted amount when the amount differs from the regular one or Adjusted otherwise, Last payment), the amount coloured by sign, then planned and balance-after.
- Grace period: hovering a transaction of a payment with a grace period (or a tentative payment, whose whole period counts) tints the days on which it may be paid with a faint Important colour, and the tooltip adds a "Can be paid" date range, which also covers days that spill into the next month. The payment editor's More schedule options has a "Grace period · days" field (0 to 366, 0 turns it off), disabled while Tentative is checked.
- Business-days payments that repeat every day or every 2 days show their Saturday and Sunday occurrences added into the Monday (or Friday with early pay) transaction, marked "adjusted", so the calendar, the plan comparison and loan payoff agree.
